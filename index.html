Resources & Frequently Asked Questions

This page provides helpful information about common legal questions and processes. Use these resources to better understand our services and prepare for your consultation.

What is Conveyancing?

Conveyancing is the legal process of transferring ownership of immovable property from one party to another. It involves drafting a deed of sale, conducting title searches, ensuring compliance with regulations, and registering the property transfer. Our firm manages all stages of the process, including communication with banks and deeds offices.

Why Plan Your Estate?

Estate planning ensures that your assets are distributed according to your wishes and that your loved ones are taken care of. Drafting a valid will, creating trusts and nominating beneficiaries can reduce disputes and estate duty. We advise on drafting wills and establishing trusts to protect your legacy.

What is an Ante-Nuptial Contract?

An ante-nuptial contract (ANC) is an agreement entered into before marriage to specify how a couple's assets and liabilities will be managed. It can exclude community of property and help protect individual assets. Our firm drafts and registers ANCs to meet your needs and comply with South African law.
